Sentinel · Drift Engine

KYC drift detection for FINMA-regulated banks: it catches customers whose risk profile is slowly becoming invalid, before a sanctions event. A 7-layer engine (Bayesian changepoint detection, drift velocity, ownership-graph contagion, causal drift and suspicious-stability checks) fuses public signals with internal bank data. In validation it flagged risk 2–7 months ahead with no false positives on stable customers. A rules→ML→LLM cascade cut costs by 96% compared with running an LLM on every customer. Every score is explainable, confirmed by a compliance officer, and written to an immutable audit log.
